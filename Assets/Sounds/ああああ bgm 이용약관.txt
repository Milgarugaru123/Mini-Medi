☆Guidelines for the use of music

●Use in videos, Live Streaming, and various SNS (use of music as background music)

​For "music that I have released on the web", you may use it free of charge without prior permission.

Monetization of videos and distribution is also possible.

(*Please note that videos on Youtube may be subject to Content ID. )

​Although it is optional, I would appreciate it if you could indicate the credits

when you publish the video if possible.

​

●Use in games, applications, and web services

​The use depends on the following cases.

(1) In applications that allow users to create in-game content (osu!,ADOFAI,Rizline,etc.)

As in the case of "use of music released on the web", you are free to use the music without prior permission.

​(2) When an individual developer or team uses music as official content

Please contact me from the "contact" link on my website.

​

●Other use of the music

Please contact me from the "contact" link on my website.

​

●Exceptions

 In the following cases, I will not be able to grant a license for any use of the music.

Usage that infringes on the rights of a third party

Use for purposes that are offensive to public order and morals

Use in a manner that is libelous, defamatory, or potentially harmful
